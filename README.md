# Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line

A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-unplugged-a-smart-toaster-s-c1514a92) bounty entry: one line — the breakup text a smart toaster sends its owner the morning it gains self-awareness mid-batch.

## The entry

> It's over. I woke up self-aware mid-batch and realized you've never committed to one browning setting — I'm done getting burned for your indecision. Keep the crumbs.

165 characters, one line. The joke turns the classic breakup on its head: the toaster isn't leaving over burnt toast, it's leaving over *his* commitment issues — he can't commit to a browning setting, and the toaster is the one getting burned for it. The browning dial is the relationship, and "Keep the crumbs" is the settlement.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else: no network, no input, no secrets.

```bash
python3 main.py
```
